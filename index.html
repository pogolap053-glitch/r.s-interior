About the Studio

Design Is a Dialogue
Between Space & Soul

MAISON is a boutique design studio founded on the belief that great interiors are not decorated — they are crafted. We listen before we draw, understand before we propose, and refine until every element feels inevitable.

Our team of 12 specialists brings together expertise in architecture, art history, material science, and lighting to create spaces of lasting beauty and meaning.

How We Work

Our Process

01

Discovery

A deep conversation about your life, aesthetics, habits, and aspirations. We explore reference images, visit the space, and define the project scope together.

02

Concept

We develop a cohesive design direction — moodboards, spatial layouts, material palettes, and 3D visualisations — refined through collaborative feedback.

03

Execution

We manage every detail — contractor coordination, custom fabrication, procurement, and quality control — so you can trust the process completely.

04

Reveal

The final styling and art placement transforms the space into its full expression. We hand over a home that feels entirely, perfectly yours.
